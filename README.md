# Road Accident Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing road accident casualty data to identify accident patterns, severity levels, vehicle involvement, road type risks, geographic hotspots, environmental impacts, and long-term trends.

The analysis was performed using Python and Pandas for data cleaning and analysis, while Microsoft Excel was used to build an interactive dashboard for visual reporting and business decision support.

The objective of this project is to help transportation authorities understand the factors contributing to road accidents and support data-driven road safety planning.

---

# Business Problem

A national transport safety authority is facing a serious issue with increasing road accidents across different regions. However, they do not clearly understand:

* Where accidents are happening most
* When accidents are happening
* What conditions lead to severe accidents
* Which factors increase risk (weather, road type, time, etc.)

---

# Business Goal

The goal of this analysis is to:

* Identify key patterns, risk factors, and high-accident zones to help reduce road accidents and improve public safety policies.

---

# What the Authority Wants to Achieve

By the end of this analysis, the organization should be able to:

* Reduce accident frequency in high-risk areas
* Understand when accidents are most likely to occur
* Identify conditions that lead to severe accidents
* Improve road safety planning and enforcement
* Support data-driven decision making for traffic control

# Key Insights

## Overall Impact

* Total casualties recorded: 896,547

## Casualties by Severity

* Slight injuries: 750,625
* Serious injuries: 129,439
* Fatal injuries: 16,483

## Casualties by Vehicle Type

* Cars were involved in 694,866 casualties, representing the largest share of all vehicle types.

## Casualties by Road Type

* Single carriageway roads accounted for 661,758 casualties, making them the highest-risk road environment.

## Geographic Risk Analysis

* Birmingham recorded the highest number of casualties among all districts.

## Urban vs Rural Comparison

* Urban areas accounted for 543,021 casualties.
* Rural areas accounted for 353,497 casualties.

## Light Conditions

* 649,874 casualties occurred during daylight conditions.
* 246,673 casualties occurred during nighttime conditions.

## Yearly Trend

* Road casualties declined steadily from 2019 to 2022.

## Combined Risk Factors

* The highest number of casualties occurred in accidents involving cars on single carriageway roads during daylight conditions.

# Executive Summary

A total of 896,547 casualties were analyzed to identify the major factors contributing to road accidents.

The analysis revealed that:

* Slight injuries accounted for the majority of casualties.
* Cars were involved in the highest number of casualties.
* Single carriageway roads were the most high-risk road type.
* Birmingham recorded the highest casualty count.
* Urban areas experienced more casualties than rural areas.
* Most casualties occurred during daylight conditions.
* Road casualties declined consistently from 2019 to 2022.
* Cars on single carriageway roads during daylight represented the most common high-risk scenario.

These findings provide valuable insights for improving road safety planning, enforcement strategies, and accident prevention initiatives.
